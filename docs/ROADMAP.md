# Product Roadmap

This roadmap is directional and intentionally omits private implementation details, real athlete data, and confidential pilot information.

## Completed foundation

- Problem definition and single-athlete product hypothesis
- Calendar-aware daily planning
- Athlete readiness and pain inputs
- Throwing workload controls
- Coach planning and overrides
- Progress and engagement views
- Mobile-first installable experience
- Synthetic public demonstration
- Product, architecture, safety, privacy, and release documentation

## Current stage: multi-athlete pilot readiness

- Platform administrator role
- Athlete, guardian, coach, and player-account identities
- Recipient-bound invitations
- Invitation expiration and single-use controls
- Guardian policy acceptance
- Coach-to-athlete assignment
- Stronger environment and data separation
- Pilot package, support expectations, and legal review preparation

## Next

### Coach operating experience

- Multi-athlete dashboard
- Exception and readiness triage
- Plan assignment and override workflows
- Team calendar management
- Athlete switching and role-aware navigation

### Pilot learning

- Onboarding completion
- Daily assignment usefulness
- Athlete completion behavior
- Coach review frequency
- Guardian clarity and trust
- Support burden and failure points

### Commercial foundation

- Pricing and packaging validation
- Subscription and entitlement design
- Payment integration
- Support and cancellation workflows
- Data retention and deletion controls
- Business, insurance, privacy, and terms review

## Later hypotheses

These are not committed features:

- Team-level products
- Instructor or facility accounts
- Additional sports
- Advanced performance analytics
- Automated plan recommendations across larger datasets
- External wearable or training-system integrations

## Prioritization rule

Work is prioritized in this order:

1. Youth safety and privacy
2. Correct access and identity boundaries
3. Daily athlete usefulness
4. Coach operating efficiency
5. Pilot learning
6. Monetization
7. Broader feature expansion
