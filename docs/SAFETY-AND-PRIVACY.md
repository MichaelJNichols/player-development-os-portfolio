# Safety and Privacy Boundary

Player Development OS concerns minor athletes and may involve readiness, pain, workload, sleep, nutrition, weight, schedules, and performance information. Those facts materially change how the product and this portfolio are structured.

## Public portfolio rules

This repository must not contain:

- real minor-athlete names or contact information;
- birth dates, school or team affiliations tied to an identifiable athlete;
- private calendar URLs or event feeds;
- passwords, tokens, invitation codes, or credentials;
- production database records or exports;
- real pain, weight, sleep, nutrition, readiness, or performance histories;
- screenshots containing identifiable youth information;
- private pilot communications or agreements.

## Product safeguards represented in the work

- Role-based access
- Guardian participation and policy acceptance
- Recipient-bound invitations
- Expiring and single-use invitation flows
- Wrong-recipient rejection
- Pain and readiness gating
- Workload controls
- Coach overrides
- Synthetic public demonstration data
- Separation between public demo and private production environments

## What the product does not claim

The product is not represented as medical advice, diagnosis, rehabilitation, or a replacement for qualified coaching or healthcare judgment. Safety rules reduce obvious risk but do not guarantee that an activity is appropriate for every athlete.

## Portfolio review standard

Before any screenshot, case study, export, or additional artifact is added publicly, it should pass two questions:

1. Could this identify a real minor directly or through context?
2. Could this reveal private health, schedule, account, family, or performance information?

If either answer may be yes, the artifact should not be published until it is rebuilt with synthetic information.
